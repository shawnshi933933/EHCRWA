name: RWA asset comparison
description: Product-level rules for grouping and comparing tokenized-equity wrappers across issuers and chains.
---

Group tokenized stock products by underlying equity only for search and discovery. Different issuers and chains may represent different legal wrappers, rights, dividends, rebasing or share-per-token mechanics, redemption paths, eligibility, and liquidity; never describe them as interchangeable solely because they track the same stock.

**Why:** The user emphasized in the product outline that TSLAon, TSLAx, and TSLAb are issued and packaged differently, are not directly 1:1 exchangeable, and may use different price mechanisms and regional restrictions.

**How to apply:** Keep issuer, chain, contract verification, price basis, share-equivalent ratio, fees, liquidity, and eligibility visible as separate fields. Clearly distinguish token price from normalized underlying exposure and mark unknown facts as unverified.
